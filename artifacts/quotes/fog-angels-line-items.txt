QUOTATION DETAILS — WINDIES MEDIA LTD. TO FOG ANGELS LTD.

Project: Fog Angels Carnival companion application
Base development and Carnival delivery package: TT$15,000
The items below form one package; they are not additional charges.

INCLUDED LINE ITEMS

1. Application design and Fog Angels branding
   Mobile-friendly web application with Fog Angels branding, responsive layouts and clear navigation.

2. Find Your Friend
   Permission-based location sharing between invited/approved friends, a map view of participating friends, and controls to stop sharing. Location sharing is limited to the agreed event use; people are not publicly discoverable by default.

3. Find a Truck
   Live truck map, authorized crew location sharing, last-update information, delayed/signal-lost indicators, and controls to start and end public tracking.

4. Push Notifications
   Organizer controls for sending event announcements and alerts to users who have opted in, including notification permission setup for supported devices and browsers.

5. Photo Gallery
   Event photo gallery with authorized upload/management, mobile browsing and full-image viewing. Optional AI face matching is a separately priced tenant add-on, as described below.

6. Administration, testing and deployment
   Authorized organizer/crew access, setup of the agreed features, functional testing, deployment and an organizer handover/walkthrough.

7. Hosting for Carnival
   Application hosting for the agreed Carnival period is included in the TT$15,000 package, within the user, traffic and storage capacity agreed for that event.

8. Event-day technical support
   Technical support for the agreed event day and agreed support hours is included in the TT$15,000 package.

PAYMENT MILESTONES

50% down payment on acceptance / before commencement: TT$7,500
30% on delivery of the completed application against the agreed feature scope: TT$4,500
20% on event day: TT$3,000
Total: TT$15,000

ADDITIONAL CHARGES — OUTSIDE THE TT$15,000 PACKAGE

- Optional AI face matching: activation/integration for the Fog Angels tenant is quoted separately. This enables consenting users to find potential photos of themselves within the event gallery.
- AI credits and processing: no AI credit allowance or unlimited AI usage is included. Matching, indexing and any other AI processing are charged under an agreed credit package or usage rate, with a spending limit approved before activation.
- Additional AI implementation, custom workflows, integrations or features beyond the agreed scope are separately quoted before work begins.
- Post-Carnival hosting is charged separately under an agreed hosting/maintenance arrangement. The included hosting period ends on the agreed date; continued hosting is not included indefinitely.

CLARIFICATIONS TO SET BEFORE ISSUING THE QUOTATION

Confirm VAT treatment; Carnival hosting start/end dates; event-day support hours and delivery mode; expected users and number of trucks; gallery storage/retention; delivery/acceptance date; and rates for optional AI and post-Carnival hosting. Do not describe the service as unlimited unless a specific unlimited item is intentionally offered.

DELIVERY NOTE FOR WINDIES MEDIA (not a quotation line item)

The current demo has live phone-based truck tracking and a curated brand-image preview. Find Your Friend, push notifications, the production upload gallery and optional AI face matching must be included in the delivery checklist; do not describe them as already implemented based on the current demo alone.
